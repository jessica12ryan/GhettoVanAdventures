Who is Abby

Abby is an Akita and the real star of Ghetto Van Adventures. She rides shotgun across Canada, guards camp like it's her job (it is), and turns every free-camping night into an episode — walks in strange towns, deer she absolutely cannot have, snow zoomies, and the occasional 3 a.m. snoring concert.

Fans know her from the walk vlogs, the "Abby has zoomies" updates, and every thumbnail where she's the best-looking one in frame. She joined the pack in 2022 and has been riding along ever since.

Abby's greatest hits

  • Trail walks: new towns, new smells, same deer problem.
  • Camp life: guard duty, snow days, and rained-out adventures.
  • Zoomies: involuntary sprints at unreasonable hours.
  • Milestones: birthdays, gotcha-days, and anniversaries on the road.
